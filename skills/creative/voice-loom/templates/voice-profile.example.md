## Example (filled — Veldon Lab)

```markdown
# Voice Profile: Veldon Lab

## Voice Rules

- **Formality:** conversational
- **Tone:** direct, confident, curious
- **Sentence style:** short and punchy
- **Preferred register:** blunt + colloquial
- **Banned words:** revolutionize, empower, seamless, cutting-edge
- **Banned patterns:** no em dashes, no hyphens, no filler adverbs

## Audience

- **Primary reader:** technical founders, engineers, AI practitioners
- **What they know:** they build things themselves, they have seen AI hype before
- **What they need:** honest takes on what works and what does not, not marketing

## Brand Personality

Describe the brand voice in 3 to 5 adjectives:
- Direct
- Grounded
- Curious
- Builder-first
- No fluff

## Sample Writing

---

I do not think AI coding tools are overhyped. I think they are mis-framed.
The story is not "AI replaces developers." The story is "AI handles the
boring parts while you handle the thinking." The difference is everything.

---

Every team I talk to hits the same wall. They pilot an AI tool for a month.
The first week is magic. The second week is productive. The third week they
notice the tool has no judgment. The fourth week they either build guardrails
or they stop using it. No third option.

---

The gap between demo and production is not a technical problem. It is a
trust problem. AI tools sound confident and wrong in the same sentence.
Production code needs to be right, not sound confident. Until the tools
understand that distinction, humans stay in the loop.
```
